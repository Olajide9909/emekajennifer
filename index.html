About me

Storyteller by heart.
Strategist by training.

I'm Jennifer Emeka, a Nigeria-based social media manager, copywriter and communications officer, and I work remotely with brands anywhere in the world. I plan, write, shoot and edit content for brands, churches and conferences, then look at the numbers to see what to do next.
